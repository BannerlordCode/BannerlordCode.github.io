## 5. The population is wrong, and the error is not random — **this is the most serious finding**

### 5.1 MEASURED

| | count |
|---|---|

**2,239 − 217 = 2,022.** The shard's population is the exact-marker set with the 217
action-variant pages removed.

### 5.2 What those 217 pages are

The generator emits **sibling self-declaration wordings** that the two exact marker strings miss:

- `"ApplyHeirSelectionAction 的自动生成战役动作参考。"` — **96 ZH pages**
- `"Auto-generated campaign action reference for ApplyHeirSelectionAction."` — **124 EN pages**
  (217 unique pages, 59 distinct action type names, all under `api/campaign-ext/`)

I read three of them. All are unambiguously generated, with machine-level evidence:

- `v1.3.0/en/api/campaign-ext/GiveGoldAction.md` →
  `**Purpose:** Applies the effect of between characters to the this instance.`
  The template split the camelCase method name into words. Nobody writes that by hand.
- `v1.3.15/en/api/campaign-ext/EndMercenaryServiceAction.md` → `**Purpose:** Executes the EndByDefault logic.`
  The intro promises mods call its `Apply*` overloads; **none of the three listed methods is named `Apply*`.**
- `v1.3.0/zh/api/campaign-ext/LiftSiegeAction.md` → `**用途 / Purpose:** 读取并返回当前对象中 game action 的结果。`
  while the only member listed, `GetGameAction`, is not an action method at all.

### 5.3 Why this refutes the shard's central inference

The shard concludes:

> *"0 of those 120 generated pages contain `Auto-generated` or `自动生成`… The marker is a total
> blind spot… absence of the marker means nothing. It is not weak evidence, it is zero evidence."*

That inference does not follow. The literal count is true (§0), but **the 217 pages that would
have refuted it were removed from the population before sampling began.** The correct conclusion
is the near-opposite:

> The exact two-string test has **terrible recall** here — it misses 217 self-declared generated
> pages. A description-level test for `/自动生成|auto-?generated/i` is a **high-precision**
> generator detector in this region: it fires on 217 genuinely generated pages, with a small and
> *identifiable* false-positive set (the pages whose *subject* is generation).

### 5.4 The brief's Rule 2, and where it actually bites

Rule 2 says do not count legitimate hand-written pages whose subject is generation as marker
hits. That is right, and I confirmed the trap is live — these are hand-written and a broad regex
matches all of them:

- `v1.3.15/en/architecture/noise-policy.md`, `v1.4.5/en/architecture/noise-policy.md`,
  `v1.3.15/zh/architecture/noise-policy.md` — the policy page about which types count as noise.
- `api/save-system/AutoGeneratedSaveManager.md`, `SaveCodeGenerationContext.md` — real pages
  *about* an AutoGenerated type.

But note the direction: the shard over-corrected and used the broad pattern to **define the
population**, which silently deleted 217 true positives from the audit. **A false positive in a
detector should cost you a page you must read by hand; using it to define the population costs
you 217 pages nobody will ever read.** (The noise-policy pages *are* in their 227 sample, so the
filter they actually applied was narrower than `/自动生成|auto-?generated/i` — see §6.3.)

### 5.5 The true population is larger than briefed
The brief cited ~2,022 (and earlier ~2,012). The correct exact-marker figure for these three
trees is **2,239**. The ±10→±217 discrepancy is not noise to be averaged away; it is 217
identified, self-declared, genuinely generated pages currently **absent from the rewrite queue**.
